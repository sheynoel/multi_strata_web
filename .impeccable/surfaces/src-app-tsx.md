# Landing page (src/App.tsx)

Scope: the single-page marketing site and its overlays (chat drawer, modals). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Philippine enterprise operations and finance leaders deciding whether Multistrata can run their workflows; actions are Book a Demo, Get Started / plan selection, brochure request, or talking to Jarvis.

Constraints: owner pinned gold on black, the reactor orb as centre of attention, and "remove nothing": every section, feature, interaction and line of copy stays; layouts and treatments may be recomposed. Metrics and testimonials are placeholders (see PRODUCT.md).

Memorable moment: clicking the orb starts Jarvis speaking and the dial's minute-track indices light in sequence like a running chronograph.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The orb is the dial and the page is its watch face; every metric is a sub-register read against one printed minute track. Refuses the glowing-HUD default: neon halos, glass cards, gradient text, pill eyebrows, three identical cards.

OWN-WORLD: Black lacquer dial ground (#0b0b0c) with a faint sunray brush radiating from the orb; applied gold indices as polished metal (#c9a24c to #e8cf8a), ivory dial printing (#f2efe8); hairline printed tracks; circles are instruments, everything else 6px; Archivo throughout, small-caps dial printing for labels; only the orb emits light.

STORY: Visitor meets Jarvis as the dial, reads the promise across it, sees the three strata as a movement in sequence, compares peso tiers on one spec sheet, then books a demo or asks Jarvis.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Orb at ~300px inside a 520px printed minute track, dead centre; "Your Operations," set right-aligned at 9 o'clock, "on Autopilot." at 3 o'clock; speak chip on the track at 6; subline and two CTAs centred below; Book a Demo top right.
